Present perfect with since / for

7 errors

Spanish uses the present with "desde". English needs present perfect when the action started in the past and continues.

I work here since 2021 → I've been working here since 2021
Box 1 · due today

Prepositions after verbs

5 errors

depend on, arrive at, explain something to someone, listen to. Direct translation from Spanish is the trap.

it depends of the client → it depends on the client
Box 2 · due tomorrow

Third person -s

4 errors

You know the rule. It disappears when you speak fast. This one is a fluency drill, not a grammar lesson.

Box 3 · due in 3 days

Active vs passive in technical explanations

2 errors

Interviewers prefer "the queue processes the data" over "the data is processed by the queue". Shorter, clearer, more confident.

the data is processed by a queue → a queue processes the data
